Privacy Policy

At SANSKRUTI BY - BANI (a brand under BLUENOTES), we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

1 Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you interact with our website:

  • Name, phone number, and email address
  • Shipping and billing address
  • Payment details (processed securely via payment gateways)
  • Order history and browsing preferences

2 How We Use Your Information

Your information is strictly used to:

  • Process and deliver your orders accurately
  • Communicate order updates and shipment tracking
  • Improve our products, customer service, and shopping experience
  • Send promotional offers and seasonal updates (only if you opt-in)

3 Payment Security

We use secure and trusted payment gateways. Your credit card, debit card, or net banking credentials are processed with bank-level encryption and are never stored on our servers.

4 Sharing of Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data to third parties. Your information may be shared only with:

  • Trusted courier partners for order delivery
  • Verified payment service providers for transaction processing

5 Cookies

Our website uses cookies to enhance your browsing experience, remember shopping cart items, and analyze overall site performance. You can disable cookies in your browser settings if preferred.

6 Data Protection

We take appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

7 Your Rights

  • Access your personal data held by us.
  • Request correction or deletion of your data at any time.

8 Changes to Policy

We may update this privacy policy periodically. Any changes will be posted immediately on this page with an updated revision date.
